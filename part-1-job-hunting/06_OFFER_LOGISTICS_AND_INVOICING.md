# 1.12 Pre-Offer Logistics, Global Payments & Invoicing

## 1. Employment Models: Independent Contractor vs. Employer of Record (EOR)

| Feature | Independent Contractor (B2B) | Employer of Record (Deel, Remote, Oyster) |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal Relationship** | Direct business-to-business contract with the foreign company. | Legally employed by the EOR's Nigerian registered entity. |
| **Payment Mechanism** | You send monthly/bi-weekly invoices; company pays via USD wire, Grey, Geegpay, or crypto. | Salary is processed locally in NGN into your standard Nigerian bank account. |
| **Taxes & Statutory Deductions** | Self-managed; responsible for personal income tax filing and reporting. | Handled automatically by EOR (PAYE, pension, health insurance). |
| **Flexibility & Rate Negotiation** | Highest flexibility; rates can be negotiated directly in USD/EUR. | Standard localized employment package with local benefits. |

## 2. International Payment Channels (Tested & Verified)

For contractor arrangements, Jesse maintains multiple redundant payment channels:

1. **Geegpay (by Raenest):**
   - Provides virtual USD, GBP, and EUR IBAN accounts.
   - Low transfer fees; instantaneous conversion to NGN or withdrawal to Nigerian Domiciliary accounts.
2. **Grey Finance:**
   - Provides dedicated foreign virtual accounts (ACH, SEPA, UK Sort Code).
   - Direct integration for fast Naira withdrawals.
3. **Payoneer:**
   - Established enterprise contractor channel accepted by global platforms (Upwork, Deel, Remote).
4. **Direct USD Wire to Nigerian Domiciliary Account:**
   - SWIFT wire directly to a local bank (e.g. GTBank, Zenith, Access Bank USD Domiciliary Account).
