# 1.8 Cold Message Library & Outreach Playbook

All templates strictly adhere to the golden rules: **Under 120 words**, zero self-centered phrases ("I hope this finds you well", "I am a passionate fast learner"), **specific hook**, one solid link, and a small, direct ask.

---

## 1. Template: Direct to Founder / CEO (v1.0)
*Target: Founders and Engineering Leads at Seed / Series A startups (<30 people).*

```text
Hi {{FirstName}}, I saw {{CompanyName}}'s recent launch of {{SpecificFeatureOrNews}} — the {{SpecificObservation}} caught my attention.

I am a Product Design Engineer (WAT / UTC+1) who builds complete mobile and full-stack software end-to-end. I recently built Rota, a React Native scheduling app with integer-based seat proration and quiet-hours-aware push notification queues. 

Here is a 90-second demo of the architecture: {{DemoLink}}

Are you hiring mobile or product engineers this quarter? If so, I'd welcome 15 minutes to share how I'd approach {{RelevantCompanyProblem}}.

Best,
Jesse Uwodi
```

---

## 2. Template: Engineer Referral Request (v1.0)
*Target: Senior / Staff Engineers and Engineering Managers on LinkedIn.*

```text
Hi {{FirstName}}, I read your recent post on {{SpecificTechnicalTopic}} at {{CompanyName}}. Your approach to {{SpecificDetail}} was insightful — I tackled a similar challenge with {{RelatedConcept}} on my own project.

I’m applying for the {{RoleTitle}} role at {{CompanyName}}. Here is a mobile project I built that mirrors what your team is shipping: {{DemoOrRepoLink}}

Would you be open to a brief 10-minute chat about the team's engineering culture? Happy to send my resume if you feel I’d be a strong referral candidate.

Thanks,
Jesse Uwodi
```

---

## 3. Template: Follow-Up #1 (4 Days Later)
*Rule: Always introduce new engineering value or shipped progress. Never say "Just checking in".*

```text
Hi {{FirstName}}, following up in case my note got lost in the shuffle. 

Since my last message, I implemented offline mutation rollbacks and keyset cursor pagination to improve feed performance under high load: {{ShortClipOrCommitLink}}. 

Still very keen to talk if product/mobile engineering is on {{CompanyName}}'s roadmap this quarter.

Best,
Jesse
```

---

## 4. Template: Follow-Up #2 (7 Days After Follow-Up #1)
*Rule: Professional closing note that leaves the door open and ceases further outreach.*

```text
Hi {{FirstName}}, last note from me. If the timing isn't right or you're not looking to expand the engineering team currently, no worries at all.

I'll continue following {{CompanyName}}'s progress with {{ProductArea}}. Wishing you and the team continued success with the launch.

Best,
Jesse
```

---

## 5. Cold Outreach Anti-Patterns ("Never Do These")

- ❌ Never start with *"I hope this email finds you well"* or *"My name is Jesse and I am writing to..."*
- ❌ Never ask for *"any opportunity"* — it indicates you haven't identified their specific problem.
- ❌ Never mention personal hardship or financial need — hiring is an economic business decision.
- ❌ Never send a generic blast with only the company name swapped in.
- ❌ Never attach a heavy PDF CV in the initial message unless explicitly requested.
- ❌ Never send more than two follow-ups without a response.
